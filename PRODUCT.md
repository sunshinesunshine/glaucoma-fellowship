# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Glaucoma fellows (SS, MN) and hospital supervisors/staff at Metta Pracharak Hospital (Wat Rai Khing). They use this portal in busy clinical environments to track rotations, verify surgical logs, coordinate on-call duties, and study for board reviews.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a high-efficiency logistics center, logbook, and learning platform for the Glaucoma Fellowship Program. It helps track surgical quotas, visual analytics, and study progress with zero friction.

## Brand Personality
Clinical, Professional, High-Efficiency. A clean, premium slate dark mode theme that conveys trust, order, and scientific rigor.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered or cramped layouts that overflow on mobile viewports.
- Non-standard, low-contrast text (e.g. muted gray on dark gray) that is hard to read in bright clinic rooms.
- Overly decorative elements like side-stripe accents, text gradients, or excessive card rounding (radii > 16px).

## Design Principles
1. **Clinical Utility First**: The interface must prioritize readability and clear visual hierarchy under high-glare or fast-paced hospital conditions.
2. **Seamless Coordination**: Navigation, filters, and logs must be extremely responsive, keeping interaction friction to an absolute minimum.
3. **Data Integrity & Trust**: Clean layout choices and robust validation states that make fellows feel confident their entries are secure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios must meet WCAG AA standards (>= 4.5:1 for normal text).
- Font sizes and touch targets must remain comfortable for quick mobile usage.
- Reduced motion support by avoiding unnecessary decorative animations.
